    How it works

    Get sound in

    • + Add > Import a file takes music, voice memos, videos (their sound), MIDI files and Tapehead project files.
    • Record (the red dot up top) records onto the armed track. In the Claude app you can record a video or pick a voice memo instead.
    • + Add > + MIDI track adds an instrument track. Double-tap its lane to make a clip, then draw notes. Pick from 60 instruments or 12 drum kits in Devices.
    • Sound maker builds lasers, whooshes, risers and more from scratch.
    • Keys (next to Sound maker, and under the notes of a MIDI clip) plays the instrument track on a piano or 16 drum pads. Turn on Write to put what you play into the clip: step by step when stopped, or as you play along.

    In the arrangement

    • Tap a clip to select it. Drag it to move it, even onto another track.
    • Drag a clip's edge to trim it.
    • Drag across empty space to select time. Tap to place the marker; playback starts there.
    • Pinch to zoom, or drag the strip under the tracks to move around.
    • Swipe the track names on the left to scroll the page.

    Transport

    • Play starts at the marker. Stop goes back to the marker; press it again to go to the start.
    • Loop repeats the time selection. Snap lines things up with the bar grid.
    • Each track has an on/off number, S to solo, and a red dot to arm it for recording. ⋯ beside its name renames, duplicates, moves or deletes it.

    Shape the sound

    • Clip: gain, fades, transpose, split, reverse. For MIDI clips, the piano roll.
    • Devices: the instrument and effects for the selected track, or Master. Add an effect lists 24, each with a line saying what it does; Effect racks has 47 ready-made chains. Order matters; save your own chains as racks.
    • Mixer: volume and pan for every track.
    • Made a mistake? Undo is up top and in every confirmation message.

    Piano roll

    • Tap an empty spot to add a note. Tap a note to select it; tap again to delete it.
    • Drag a note to move it; drag its right edge to change its length.
    • Drag empty space to scroll. Tap the keys to hear them.

    Save and share

    • Your work is kept on this device automatically. Settings > Data > Projects holds named copies; Backup and restore keeps everything in one file.
    • Share (next to + Add) opens its own page: it renders the song and opens the share sheet: AirDrop, Messages, Files.
    • Press and hold: the menu button jumps straight to any page or Settings group; a track opens its options; inside a clip, hold then drag to highlight a section for Copy, Cut, Duplicate and more; Play plays from the start; Loop sets the loop markers; Undo, Redo and zoom repeat; a setting resets to its default; a Sound maker preset goes straight onto a new track.
    • Session view (the switch above the tracks, as in Ableton): tracks across, scenes down. Tap a clip to launch it on the next bar; it loops until you tap it again. ▶ launches a whole scene; ■ stops a track. Hold a slot to copy a clip in, edit it, or send it to the arrangement.
    • Loop markers: Loop plays between them. Drag their ends in the ruler, or the middle of the bar to move both; Set loop to selection puts them on the time selection.
    • Reload: pull down from the very top of the page and let go, then tap Reload Tapehead. Your work is saved first.
    • Menu: tap ☰ in the control bar, or swipe in from the right edge of the screen, to go to any part of Tapehead. Swipe it back to the right or tap outside it to close.
    • Settings has five groups: Data, Audio, Edit, Look and App. Each setting says what it does.
    • On a computer: Space plays or stops, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes, Ctrl/Cmd+E splits, Ctrl/Cmd+D duplicates, Ctrl/Cmd+T adds a track (Shift for MIDI), Ctrl/Cmd+L loops, F9 records.

    Share or export

    Make a file of your song to send or keep

    Everything that is playing goes in: all audible tracks, their devices and the Master devices. In the Claude app on iPhone this opens the iOS share sheet, so you can AirDrop it, send it in Messages, save it to Files, or open it in another app.

    Projects

    Your songs, saved on this device

    A project keeps every track, clip, device and setting. Projects live on this device, and your current work is kept automatically too. Share project file sends a .zip you can open on another device with Open project file.

    Backup and restore

    One file with everything on this phone: every project and its audio, your racks, sounds, themes and settings. Keep it in Files or iCloud Drive; open it on a new phone to carry on.

    Install on your phone
    1. Open the web address above in Safari.
    2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
    3. Tapehead opens full-screen from its icon and works offline. On the website the microphone and the share sheet work directly.
    4. A Home Screen app has no reload button: pull down from the top of the page and let go, then tap Reload Tapehead. Settings and the help panel have Reload too.
